OUR APPROACH

Education first.

Scientific information can be approachable without overstating what the evidence shows.

Peppy Little Secret presents research information in a clean, understandable format — while being careful to distinguish experimental, preclinical research from established medical evidence. We're not a pharmacy, a manufacturer, or a clinic; we're an educational resource for people who want to understand the research landscape around peptides and related compounds.

Who we are

Peppy Little Secret is an educational research library covering peptides and compounds studied in scientific research. We organize information by area of interest — metabolic research, tissue recovery, cellular health, and more — and clearly distinguish what's investigational from what's FDA-approved.

Our goal is simple: make research literacy accessible. Not hype, not fear — just clear, organized information so you can understand what's actually being studied and why. For the story behind why we started this, read Why Peppy Little Secret Exists in our Journal.

How we handle evidence

Every entry in our Research Library distinguishes between laboratory and preclinical research, early human research, larger clinical trials, and established, FDA-reviewed evidence. Where a compound is FDA-approved, we say so — and specify exactly what it's approved for, since approval for one use doesn't extend to others. Where a compound is purely investigational or has no approved human or veterinary use, we say that clearly too.

What we don't do

We don't provide dosing protocols, reconstitution instructions, administration guidance, or personal medical advice. We don't sell products, and nothing on this site is a purchasing mechanism. If you have questions about a specific compound or your own health, we'd encourage speaking with a qualified healthcare professional.
