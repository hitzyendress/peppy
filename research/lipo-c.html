Lipo-C

A name commonly used for injectable lipotropic-nutrient blends; formulations vary and are not FDA-approved as weight-loss drugs.

What is Lipo-C?

Lipo-C is a common name for lipotropic-nutrient blends, typically combining compounds like methionine, inositol, and choline (MIC), sometimes with added B-vitamins. Formulations vary between compounding sources, and the specific combination and concentration is not standardized.

Areas of Research Interest

  • Fat metabolism and lipotropic compound research
  • Liver health and detoxification pathway studies
  • Energy metabolism research
  • Nutrient-combination formulation studies

Regulatory Status

Lipo-C blends, as combined injectable formulations, are not FDA-approved as weight-loss drugs. Individual components may have different regulatory statuses on their own, but the combined blend itself has not undergone FDA drug approval.
