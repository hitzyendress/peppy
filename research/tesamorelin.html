Tesamorelin

A GHRH analog FDA-approved under the brand name Egrifta for reducing excess abdominal fat in HIV-associated lipodystrophy.

For research purposes only — not for human or veterinary use. This page is educational and not medical advice.
FDA-APPROVED FOR A SPECIFIC USE

What is Tesamorelin?

Tesamorelin is a synthetic analog of growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH). It is FDA-approved under the brand name Egrifta for the specific treatment of excess abdominal fat in HIV patients with lipodystrophy, and is studied more broadly for its effects on growth hormone signaling.

Areas of Research Interest

  • Growth hormone secretion pathway studies
  • Body composition and visceral fat research
  • Cognitive function studies in aging populations
  • Metabolic health research

Regulatory Status

Tesamorelin is FDA-approved under the brand name Egrifta specifically for reducing excess abdominal fat in HIV-associated lipodystrophy. Uses outside of this approved indication are considered off-label and are not FDA-approved.
