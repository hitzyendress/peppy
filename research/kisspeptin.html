Kisspeptin

A naturally occurring hormone studied for its central role in regulating reproductive hormone signaling.

For research purposes only — not for human or veterinary use. This page is educational and not medical advice.

What is Kisspeptin?

Kisspeptin is a naturally occurring hormone that plays a central regulatory role in the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal axis, the system controlling reproductive hormone signaling in the body. It has become a significant focus of reproductive endocrinology research.

Areas of Research Interest

  • Hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal axis regulation studies
  • Reproductive hormone signaling research
  • Fertility and reproductive health studies
  • Puberty onset research

Regulatory Status

Kisspeptin occurs naturally in the body. Research-grade or synthetic kisspeptin formulations are not FDA-approved for any human or veterinary use and are intended solely for laboratory research.
