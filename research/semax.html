Semax

A synthetic peptide developed in Russia and studied there for cognitive and neuroprotective effects; not FDA-approved.

For research purposes only — not for human or veterinary use. This page is educational and not medical advice.
NOT FDA-APPROVED FOR ANY USE

What is Semax?

Semax is a synthetic peptide fragment derived from adrenocorticotropic hormone (ACTH). It was developed and studied extensively in Russia, where it has been researched for cognitive-enhancing and neuroprotective properties, though it has not undergone FDA review.

Areas of Research Interest

  • Cognitive function and memory research
  • Neuroprotective pathway studies
  • Brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF) research
  • Focus and attention studies

Regulatory Status

Semax is not FDA-approved for any use in the United States. While it has been studied and used in Russia under different regulatory frameworks, it has not completed FDA clinical trials and is classified as a research chemical in the U.S. context.
