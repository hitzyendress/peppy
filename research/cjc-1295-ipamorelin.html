CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin

A combination of a GHRH analog (CJC-1295) and a growth-hormone secretagogue (ipamorelin), studied together for additive effects on growth hormone release.

For research purposes only — not for human or veterinary use. This page is educational and not medical advice.
NOT FDA-APPROVED FOR ANY USE

What is CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin?

This combination pairs CJC-1295, a growth-hormone releasing hormone (GHRH) analog, with ipamorelin, a selective growth-hormone secretagogue. Researchers have studied this pairing for potentially additive or synergistic effects on growth hormone release compared to either compound alone.

Areas of Research Interest

  • Synergistic growth hormone release pathway studies
  • Combination-therapy research design
  • Body composition and recovery research
  • Sleep quality studies

Regulatory Status

Neither CJC-1295 nor ipamorelin, individually or combined, are FDA-approved for any human or veterinary use. This combination has not completed human clinical trials and is classified as a research chemical intended solely for laboratory research.
