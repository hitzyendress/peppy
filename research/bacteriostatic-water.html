Bacteriostatic Water

Sterile water containing a small amount of benzyl alcohol, FDA-regulated as a multi-dose diluent for pharmaceutical and laboratory reconstitution.

For research purposes only — not for human or veterinary use. This page is educational and not medical advice.
FDA-APPROVED FOR A SPECIFIC USE

What is Bacteriostatic Water?

Bacteriostatic water is sterile water containing 0.9% benzyl alcohol, which acts as a preservative that inhibits bacterial growth. This allows it to be used as a multi-dose diluent, unlike standard sterile water which must be used once and discarded.

Areas of Research Interest

  • Reconstitution and stability studies for research compounds
  • Sterility and preservation research
  • Multi-dose formulation studies
  • Compatibility research with various peptide compounds

Regulatory Status

Bacteriostatic water is FDA-regulated as a pharmaceutical diluent. It is used for reconstituting research compounds and requires careful handling; it should not be used for reconstituting products intended for newborns due to the benzyl alcohol content.
