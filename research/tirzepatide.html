What is Formula T (Tirzepatide)?

Tirzepatide is a dual GLP-1/GIP receptor agonist. It is FDA-approved under specific brand names for type 2 diabetes management and chronic weight management, and is one of the most studied compounds in its class.

Areas of Research Interest

  • Dual-receptor agonism and glucose regulation studies
  • Body weight management clinical research
  • Cardiometabolic outcomes research
  • Long-term safety and tolerability studies

Regulatory Status

Tirzepatide is FDA-approved under specific brand names for type 2 diabetes and chronic weight management. Uses outside these approved indications, or non-branded/compounded versions, are not FDA-approved.
