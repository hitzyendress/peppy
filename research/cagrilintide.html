METABOLIC RESEARCH

Cagrilintide

A long-acting amylin analog studied in combination with GLP-1 therapies for weight management.

For research purposes only — not for human or veterinary use. This page is educational and not medical advice.
INVESTIGATIONAL — IN CLINICAL TRIALS

What is Cagrilintide?

Cagrilintide is a long-acting amylin analog. Amylin is a hormone co-secreted with insulin that plays a role in appetite regulation and glucose control. Cagrilintide is being studied particularly in combination with GLP-1 receptor agonists for potentially additive effects.

Areas of Research Interest

  • Amylin receptor pathway studies
  • Combination therapy research with GLP-1 agonists
  • Appetite regulation and satiety research
  • Body weight management clinical trials

Regulatory Status

Cagrilintide is investigational and currently undergoing clinical trials, often studied in combination with other compounds. It has not received FDA approval for any use as a standalone or combination therapy.
