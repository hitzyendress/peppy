CELLULAR & MITOCHONDRIAL RESEARCH

MOTS-c

A mitochondrial-derived peptide studied for its role in cellular energy metabolism.

For research purposes only — not for human or veterinary use. This page is educational and not medical advice.
NOT FDA-APPROVED FOR ANY USE

What is MOTS-c?

MOTS-c is a mitochondrial-derived peptide encoded within mitochondrial DNA. Discovered more recently than many research peptides, it has drawn scientific interest for its apparent role in regulating cellular energy metabolism and communication between mitochondria and the cell nucleus.

Areas of Research Interest

  • Mitochondrial function and cellular energy metabolism studies
  • Insulin sensitivity and metabolic health research
  • Exercise physiology and endurance studies
  • Aging and longevity research

Regulatory Status

MOTS-c is not FDA-approved for any human or veterinary use. It remains in early stages of scientific investigation and is classified as a research chemical intended solely for laboratory research.
