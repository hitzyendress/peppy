FROM THE FOUNDER

Why Peppy Little Secret Exists

A note on research literacy and why this site was built.

Peppy Little Secret started from a simple, frustrating problem: research on peptides and emerging compounds is everywhere online — scattered across forums, half-explained on social media, buried in dense clinical papers — but almost nowhere is it organized, honest, and easy to actually understand.

I came to this world the way a lot of people do: managing a complicated health picture, tired of feeling like I had to choose between "trust a stranger on the internet" and "read a 40-page clinical trial PDF." Living with chronic illness means you spend a lot of time researching. You learn to read labels closely, to ask what evidence actually exists, and to separate what's promising from what's just noise. That process — the research itself — became something I cared about deeply, independent of any single outcome.

So Peppy isn't built to sell you on anything. It's built to be the resource I wished existed: one place that tells you plainly what's investigational versus FDA-approved, what a compound is actually being studied for, and where the real uncertainty is — without the hype and without the fear-mongering either.

We believe research literacy matters. Understanding a compound's mechanism, its regulatory status, and the gap between animal studies and human evidence isn't gatekeeping — it's respect for the people doing their own research, on their own time, for their own reasons.

Small choices, bigger tomorrows. That's the whole idea.

— The Peppy Little Secret team
