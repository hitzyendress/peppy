FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

FAQ

What is Peppy Little Secret?
Peppy Little Secret is an educational publication about peptides and compounds studied in scientific research. We publish plain-language research summaries and a regularly updated Journal. We are not a pharmacy, retailer, or manufacturer, and we don't sell anything through this site.
What is the Research Library?
The Research Library is a topic-organized reference covering compounds that come up in research and wellness conversations — including their mechanisms, what's being studied, the quality of available evidence, and their FDA/regulatory status. It's organized by area of interest, not as a product catalog.
What does "for research purposes only" mean?
It means the compound in question has not been established, through FDA review, as safe and effective for human or veterinary use in the way it's being discussed. Some compounds on this site are approved by the FDA for a specific, different use under a specific brand name — where that's the case, we say so explicitly and distinguish it from general or off-label use.
Is this website medical advice?
No. Nothing on this site is medical advice, and none of it should be used to make decisions about your health without consulting a qualified healthcare professional. We aim to explain research clearly, not to recommend or guide personal use of any substance.
Does Peppy provide dosing or administration instructions?
No. We intentionally do not include dosing protocols, reconstitution instructions, injection or administration guidance, or cycle protocols anywhere on this site, including on individual research pages.
How can I contact Peppy?
You can reach us through the Contact page for general questions, editorial inquiries, or corrections. See our Contact page for details.
